        How to play

        Your turn. Answer a maths question, then roll. Everyone rolls the main die. A right answer adds a bonus die, so you move further.

        Your power. Each racer has one power. It switches on by itself whenever its rule is met, and the game shows you when it does.

        Special spaces. +2 boosts you 2 ahead. −2 slips you 2 back. ? is a pop quiz: get it right to win an item card.

        Item cards. You can hold 2. Shield blocks the next thing that would send you backwards, all by itself. Double move and Swap places are played at the start of your turn. In team mode, Swap places is replaced by Push back, which pushes the Eraser back 5 spaces.

        Boards. The river crossing and mountain climb have shortcuts (a teal space with an arrow): answer a harder question to jump ahead, or slide back 2 if you miss. They also have bridges: each racer who lands on one makes it creak, and the third landing collapses it, dropping everyone on it to the riverbank. After that, landing on the broken bridge drops you back too.

        Levels. With “Adjust each racer’s level as they play” switched on, 3 right answers in a row move a racer’s questions up a level, and 2 misses in a row move them down. The leaderboard shows each racer’s level.

        Race each other. The first racer across the finish line wins.

        Team up. The Eraser chases the team from the start line and wipes spaces as it goes. If it catches a racer, the team spends a stick of chalk to redraw them a little ahead. Get everyone across the line before the chalk runs out. Racers who have finished keep answering questions, and their rolls go to the teammate at the back. Powers that would push other racers back push the Eraser back instead.

        Playing on separate devices. If this copy of the game has online play switched on, one person sets up the race and taps “Let others join”. Everyone else opens the same page, types the invite code and picks a racer. The race runs on the first person’s device, so that device needs to stay open.

        Keys. Press 1, 2, 3 or 4 to pick an answer.
