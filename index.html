        How to play

        Your turn. Answer a maths question, then roll. Everyone rolls the main die. A right answer adds a bonus die, so you move further.

        Your power. Each racer has one power. It switches on by itself whenever its rule is met, and the game shows you when it does.

        Special spaces. +2 boosts you 2 ahead. −2 slips you 2 back. ? is a pop quiz: get it right to win an item card.

        Item cards. You can hold 2. Shield blocks the next thing that would send you backwards, all by itself. Double move and Swap places are played at the start of your turn. In team mode, Swap places is replaced by Push back, which pushes Evil Mr H back 5 spaces.

        Boards. The river crossing and mountain climb have shortcuts (a teal space with an arrow): answer a harder question to jump ahead, or slide back 2 if you miss. They also have bridges: each racer who lands on one makes it creak, and the third landing collapses it, dropping everyone on it to the riverbank. After that, landing on the broken bridge drops you back too.

        Levels. With “Adjust each racer’s level as they play” switched on, 3 right answers in a row move a racer’s questions up a level, and 2 misses in a row move them down. The leaderboard shows each racer’s level.

        Race each other. The first racer across the finish line wins.

        Team up. Evil Mr H chases the team from the start line, wiping the board as he goes. If he catches a racer, the team spends a stick of chalk to redraw them a little ahead. Get everyone across the line before the chalk runs out. Racers who have finished keep answering questions, and their rolls go to the teammate at the back. Powers that would push other racers back push Evil Mr H back instead. The scoreboard on the Team up card keeps count of how many times the class has beaten him, and how many times he has won.

        Playing on separate devices. If this copy of the game has online play switched on, one person sets up the race and taps “Let others join”. Everyone else opens the same page, types the invite code and picks a racer. The race runs on the first person’s device, so that device needs to stay open.

        Knockout tournament. Choose it on the home screen with 3 to 8 racers. The game draws heats, and the best racers from each go through to the final. A bracket between races shows who’s through and who’s out. Heats are quick races; the final uses your race length. It works with invite codes too.

        Adventure. Play on your own through 12 levels in three worlds: races, chases by Evil Mr H, and bosses. Win a level for a star, win with 70% right for 2 stars, and with 90% right for 3. You start with 6 racers, and every star unlocks more; 3 stars on every level unlocks all of them. Progress is saved on this device only. Bosses wear a crown and move extra spaces every turn.

        Evil Mr H’s special moves. In team-up races, every third round once he’s awake, he plays a move: Homework (everyone answers; right moves you 1 forward, wrong slips you 2 back), Detention (the leader misses a turn, unless they have a Shield), Surprise test (one racer answers for the team: right pushes him back 3, wrong lets him charge 3) or Confiscated (he takes an item card). Switch them off in Race options.

        Results. After a race, click Results to see how each player did by question type and every question they missed, with their answer and the right one. Print gives a clean page to keep.

        Board builder. In Race options, click Make your own board. Choose a style and length, then click spaces to add boosts, slips, pop quizzes, bridges and shortcuts. Saved boards appear in the Board choices. Copy share code gives a code that anyone can paste into their own board builder.

        Read aloud. Tap the speaker on any question to hear it and its answers. Switch on “Read aloud” at the top to have every question on this device read out automatically.

        Keys. Press 1, 2, 3 or 4 to pick an answer.
